## Overview
I completed a PwC-sponsored nonprofit consulting externship focused on improving communication strategy for education-focused nonprofit organizations. The project centered on helping Extra Mile strengthen outreach, fundraising communication, audience engagement, and stakeholder visibility.

This was one of my earlier consulting projects, and it helped me build experience turning research into executive-level recommendations.

## Business Problem
Extra Mile needed a stronger communication strategy to better reach families, donors, partners, and underserved communities.

The main challenges were:
- Communication gaps with target audiences
- Limited digital reach
- Need for stronger fundraising visibility
- Need for more inclusive communication
- Opportunity to improve engagement across social media channels

## Project Flow
1. Reviewed communication challenges faced by education-focused nonprofits.
2. Benchmarked similar organizations including Propel Schools Foundation, Crossroads Foundation, and Boys & Girls Clubs of Western Pennsylvania.
3. Identified key trends in nonprofit communication, fundraising, and social engagement.
4. Built recommendations around outreach, digital inclusion, partnerships, and measurement.
5. Presented a strategy deck with actionable recommendations.

## Key Trends Identified
- Collaborative fundraising was a major driver of visibility and donor engagement.
- Diversifying revenue streams helped nonprofits reduce dependence on one funding source.
- Community engagement and outreach were critical for building trust.
- Data-driven communication strategies helped organizations improve targeting and measure impact.

## Recommendations
I recommended that Extra Mile focus on four major areas:

### 1. Enhance Social Media Engagement
Use Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, reels, stories, and live video to reach different audience groups and keep stakeholders updated.

### 2. Build Collaborative Partnerships
Partner with local businesses, schools, media outlets, and community organizations to expand reach and increase credibility.

### 3. Streamline Fundraising Initiatives
Use digital campaigns, corporate matching gifts, and retail partnerships to create more consistent fundraising opportunities.

### 4. Improve Inclusive Communication
Use multilingual content, testimonials, videos, infographics, and accessible messaging to better serve diverse communities.

## Key Takeaway
The biggest takeaway was that communication strategy is not just about posting more content. It is about matching the right message, channel, and audience so the organization can build trust, increase engagement, and drive measurable support.

This project helped me build consulting, research, and presentation skills. It also strengthened my ability to benchmark organizations, identify communication gaps, and turn findings into practical recommendations that leadership could act on.
