## Featured Projects

### 1. [Labor Market & Earnings Regression Analysis](./earnings-analysis-econometrics/README.md)
- Conducted econometric analysis using ACS data to evaluate earnings differences and labor market outcomes across undergraduate majors.
- Applied **OLS regression** and statistical modeling techniques in **R** to improve model fit and reduce omitted variable bias.
- Visualized trends and earnings distributions using `ggplot2` and summary statistics.

### 2. [Consumer Insights & Sentiment Analysis – Beats by Dre](./beats-by-dre-data-analytics/README.md)
- Analyzed **4,000+ survey responses** and **5,000+ online reviews** for sentiment and feature analysis.
- Applied **Python** (pandas, nltk, vaderSentiment) for text processing and scoring.
- Built visual dashboards and delivered recommendations for product and marketing teams.

### 3. [Ticket Pricing & Revenue Optimization Analysis](./clippers-pricing-analysis/README.md)
- Built a full **SQL + Tableau pipeline** analyzing ticket pricing, attendance, resale behavior, and ancillary revenue trends across 100+ live events.
- Created interactive dashboards showing **sell-through, revenue by opponent, resale pricing behavior, and league benchmark comparisons**.
- Tools: **MySQL**, **Tableau Public**, **Excel**
- [**View Live Dashboard on Tableau Public**](https://public.tableau.com/views/ClippersPricingAnalysisDashboard/ClippersTicketPricingAttendancePerformance202425?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

### 4. [Commercial Real Estate Investment Analysis](./commercial-real-estate-investment-analysis/README.md)
- Conducted market, demographic, and financial analysis on a commercial fitness property using vacancy trends, NOI projections, ROI calculations, and IRR forecasting.
- Evaluated redevelopment opportunities and built a 5-year investment projection estimating ~92% ROI and ~13–15% IRR.
- Tools: **Excel**, **Financial Modeling**, **Market Research**

### 5. [Pepsi DCF Valuation Analysis](./pepsi-dcf-valuation-analysis/README.md)
- Built a discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation model for PepsiCo using projected revenue growth, free cash flow forecasting, and terminal value analysis.
- Conducted sensitivity analysis and valuation modeling to estimate intrinsic company value.
- Tools: **Excel**, **Financial Modeling**, **Forecasting**

### 6. [Intuit Dome Economic Impact Analysis](./intuit-dome-economic-impact-analysis/README.md)
- Designed a difference-in-differences (DiD) research framework analyzing the economic impact of the Intuit Dome development on employment, housing affordability, and business activity in Inglewood.
- Analyzed ACS, Zillow, and California labor market datasets to evaluate economic development trends and public policy outcomes.
- Tools: **Economic Research**, **Public Data Analysis**, **Excel**
